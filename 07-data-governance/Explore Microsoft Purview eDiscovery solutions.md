## Microsoft Purview eDiscovery Summary

### Overview:
- **eDiscovery (electronic discovery)** helps organizations:
  - Find
  - Preserve
  - Analyze
  - Export electronic data for **legal or compliance investigations**

- Supports data across:
  - Exchange (email)
  - SharePoint
  - OneDrive
  - Microsoft Teams
  - Microsoft 365 Groups

---

## eDiscovery Solutions in Purview:

### 1. Content Search
- Basic tool for:
  - Searching Microsoft 365 data
  - Exporting results

✅ Best for: **quick, simple searches**

---

### 2. eDiscovery (Standard)
- Builds on Content Search
- Adds **case management and legal controls**

### Key Features:
- Create and manage cases  
- Assign permissions (case-specific access)  
- Associate searches with cases  
- Place **legal holds** to preserve data  

✅ Best for: **basic investigations with legal hold**

---

### 3. eDiscovery (Premium)
- Full **end-to-end investigation solution**
- Requires:
  - Microsoft 365 E5 / Defender Plan 2

### Advanced Capabilities:
- Custodian management  
- Legal hold notifications  
- Data collection into **review sets**  
- Advanced analytics & AI  
- Predictive coding (prioritize relevant data)  
- OCR (search text in images)  
- Conversation threading  

✅ Best for: **complex legal and compliance investigations**

---

## Key Capabilities (Across Solutions):

### Core Functions:
- Search content across multiple services  
- Use keyword queries (KQL)  
- View search statistics  
- Export results  

---

### Case Management (Standard & Premium):
- Organize investigations into cases  
- Restrict access to case members only  

---

### Legal Hold:
- Preserve data during investigations  
- Prevent accidental or intentional deletion  

---

### Advanced (Premium Only):

#### Custodian Management:
- Track users involved in investigations  
- Manage their data sources  

---

#### Review Sets:
- Secure workspace for collected data  
- Enables:
  - Filtering
  - Tagging
  - Searching

---

#### Analytics:
- Near-duplicate detection  
- Email threading  
- Theme identification  

---

#### Predictive Coding:
- Uses AI to:
  - Identify relevant documents  
  - Reduce review effort  

---

#### OCR (Optical Character Recognition):
- Extracts text from images for searching  

---

#### Error Remediation:
- Fix issues with files (e.g., password-protected documents)

---

## Workflow (Premium):
1. Identify case  
2. Preserve data (legal hold)  
3. Collect relevant data  
4. Review & filter content  
5. Analyze using AI tools  
6. Export final evidence  

---

## Export Options:
- Local computer  
- Customer-managed Azure Storage  
- Includes:
  - Files
  - Metadata
  - Tags
  - Redacted documents  

---

- ✅ Centralized investigation process

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview eDiscovery provides **scalable and comprehensive tools for legal investigations**, from simple searches to advanced AI-powered analysis, enabling organizations to **efficiently identify, preserve, review, and produce critical data**.
