## Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management Summary

### Overview:
- Enables organizations to **retain, manage, and delete data** efficiently
- Supports compliance, regulatory requirements, and risk reduction
- Applies across Microsoft 365 services such as:
  - Exchange
  - SharePoint
  - OneDrive
  - Teams
  - Yammer

---

## Core Concept:
### Retention Policies (Foundation)
- Control how long data is kept and when it’s deleted
- Support:
  - ✅ Retain data for a period (or indefinitely)
  - ✅ Automatically delete data after a defined period
  - ✅ Combine both (e.g., retain for 3 years → then delete)

---

### Scope Options:
- Apply to:
  - Entire organization (all mailboxes/sites)
  - Specific users, departments, or locations

---

### Retention Labels:
- Used for **exceptions or granular control**
- Can be:
  - Applied manually by users
  - Applied automatically based on content

---

## Additional Features:

### 1. Mailbox Archiving
- Adds extra mailbox storage
- Supports **auto-expanding archives (>100 GB)**

---

### 2. Inactive Mailboxes
- Retain user data after employees leave
- Helps with compliance and investigations

---

### 3. PST Import
- Import legacy email data into Microsoft 365
- Methods:
  - Network upload
  - Drive shipping

---

## How to Get Started:

1. **Understand retention requirements**
   - Regulatory, legal, and business needs  

2. **Create retention policies**
   - Define retention duration and actions  

3. **Apply retention labels (if needed)**
   - For specific documents or exceptions  

4. **Enable mailbox archiving**
   - Provide additional storage  

5. **Manage inactive mailboxes**
   - Retain data for former employees  

6. **Import legacy data**
   - Bring PST data under governance  

---

## Permissions:
- Use roles such as:
  - **Compliance Administrator**
  - **Retention Management**
- No need for access to actual content to manage policies

---

## Benefits:
- ✅ Ensures regulatory compliance  
- ✅ Reduces legal and security risk  
- ✅ Minimizes unnecessary data storage  
- ✅ Automates data lifecycle processes  
- ✅ Improves data governance across the organization  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview Data Lifecycle Management helps organizations **retain essential data, securely delete unnecessary data, and automate governance processes**, reducing risk while ensuring compliance and efficient data management.
