## Microsoft Purview Information Protection Summary

### Overview:
- Helps organizations **discover, classify, protect, and prevent data loss**
- Applies across:
  - Emails
  - Documents
  - Cloud apps
  - Endpoints
- Core principle: **Know your data → Protect your data → Prevent data loss**

---

## 1. Know Your Data (Discovery & Classification)
- Identify where sensitive data exists

### Key Capabilities:
- **Sensitive information types**
  - Detect patterns (e.g., credit cards, SSN)
- **Trainable classifiers**
  - Use AI to identify data based on examples
- **Data classification tools**
  - Content Explorer
  - Activity Explorer

✅ Provides visibility into:
- What data exists
- Where it is located
- How it’s being used

---

## 2. Protect Your Data (Labeling & Protection)

### Core Feature: Sensitivity Labels
- Apply protection such as:
  - Encryption
  - Access restrictions
  - Visual markings (e.g., Confidential)

---

### Additional Protection Tools:
- **Office 365 Message Encryption (OME)** → Secure emails  
- **Double Key Encryption (DKE)** → Full control over encryption keys  
- **Customer Key** → Control encryption at service level  
- **SharePoint IRM** → Protect files in SharePoint  
- **Unified labeling client & scanner** → Apply labels on endpoints & on-prem data  
- **Defender for Cloud Apps** → Protect cloud data  
- **Purview Data Map** → Auto-label structured/unstructured data  
- **Information Protection SDK** → Extend to third-party apps  

---

## 3. Prevent Data Loss (DLP)

### Data Loss Prevention (DLP):
- Prevent accidental or unauthorized sharing
- Enforce policies across:
  - Email
  - Cloud apps
  - Endpoints
  - Teams

---

### DLP Capabilities:
- **Microsoft Purview DLP**
  - Detect & block sensitive data sharing
- **Endpoint DLP**
  - Protect data on devices (Windows)
- **Compliance Extension**
  - Extend protection to browsers (Chrome)
- **On-premises DLP scanner**
  - Monitor local file shares
- **Teams DLP**
  - Protect chat and channel data

---

## Key Benefits:
- ✅ Full visibility into sensitive data  
- ✅ Automated classification and labeling  
- ✅ Strong data protection (encryption + access control)  
- ✅ Prevention of accidental data leaks  
- ✅ Compliance with regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview Information Protection enables organizations to **understand, secure, and control sensitive data across all environments**, ensuring **data is protected and compliant wherever it resides or travels**.
