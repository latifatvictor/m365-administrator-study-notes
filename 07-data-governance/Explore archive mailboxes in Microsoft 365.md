## Archive Mailboxes in Microsoft 365 Summary

### Overview:
- Archive mailboxes provide a **centralized, compliant alternative to PST files**
- Help organizations:
  - Retain important email data
  - Reduce reliance on local storage
  - Improve compliance and governance

---

## How Archive Mailboxes Work:

### Admin Perspective:
- When enabled:
  - A **primary mailbox** remains active
  - A separate **archive mailbox** is created
- Both are treated as **one mailbox for compliance**:
  - Content search
  - Retention policies
  - Litigation hold

---

### User Perspective:
- Access via:
  - Outlook
  - Outlook on the web
- Users can:
  - Move/copy emails manually
  - Use automated archive policies
- No need for:
  - PST files
  - Local backups

---

## Key Benefits:
- ✅ Extra mailbox storage  
- ✅ Improved compliance and governance  
- ✅ Centralized email storage  
- ✅ No reliance on local PST files  
- ✅ Simplified data access and search  

---

## Moving Data to Archive:
- Drag-and-drop emails  
- Apply archive policies (automatic)  
- Import PST files  

---

## Default Archiving Behavior:
- Emails **older than 2 years** → moved to archive mailbox  
- Recoverable items **older than 14 days** → moved to archive  

---

## Recoverable Items Folder:
- Protects against accidental or malicious deletion
- Stores:
  - Deleted items
  - Purged items
  - Data under investigation

### Key Features:
- Used for:
  - Litigation Hold  
  - eDiscovery  
  - Audit logging  
- Not visible to users (non-IPM subtree)

---

## Storage & Performance:
- Archive mailbox not cached locally
- Requires online connection to access
- Reduces device storage usage

---

## Searching Mailboxes:
- Users can search:
  - Primary mailbox
  - Archive mailbox
- Results include **both mailboxes**

---

## Auto-Expanding Archiving:
- Default archive size: **100 GB**
- With auto-expansion: up to **1.5 TB**

---

## Compliance Advantages:
- Supports:
  - Retention policies  
  - Litigation hold  
  - eDiscovery  
- Ensures:
  - Secure data preservation
  - Controlled deletion

---

## Key Takeaway:
Archive mailboxes in Microsoft 365 provide a **secure, scalable, and compliant way to store email data**, replacing PST files while enabling **better data management, search, and regulatory compliance**.
