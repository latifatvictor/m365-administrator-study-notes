## View DLP Policy Results – Microsoft Purview Summary

### Overview:
Microsoft Purview provides **reporting and monitoring tools** to help organizations:
- ✅ Track DLP policy effectiveness  
- ✅ Investigate user activity  
- ✅ Review alerts and incidents  
- ✅ Tune and improve data protection controls  

---

## Reporting Categories:

### 1. Policy
- Shows:
  - Policy names, status, scope, and actions  
- Insights:
  - Which policies are active  
  - What data they affect  

---

### 2. Classification
- Shows:
  - Sensitivity labels and classification types  
- Insights:
  - Types of sensitive data detected  
  - Policies tied to those classifications  

---

### 3. Activity
- Shows:
  - User and system activity  
- Details include:
  - Time, user, action, source/destination  
- Insights:
  - How users interact with sensitive data  

---

### 4. Audit
- Shows:
  - Detailed logs of DLP events  
- Includes:
  - Operations performed  
  - Users involved  
- Insights:
  - Compliance tracking and investigation  

---

## Key DLP Reporting Tools:

---

### 1. DLP Alerts Dashboard

✅ Purpose:
- Central hub for **DLP alerts**

#### Capabilities:
- Configure alerts  
- Review and investigate  
- Triage incidents  
- Track resolution  

---

#### What It Displays:
- Alert details  
- Triggering activity  
- Rich metadata (user, file, location, action)  

---

### 2. Activity Explorer

✅ Purpose:
- Analyze **user activity related to sensitive data**

---

#### Features:
- Filter activity across:
  - Last 30 days  
- View:
  - File changes  
  - Label updates  
  - Policy rule matches  

---

#### Common Filters:
- Endpoint DLP activity  
- Sensitive data files  
- Data egress actions  
- Policy and rule matches  

---

### Key Insight:
- Default filter = **DLP Rule Match**
- Shows:
  - What triggered a policy  
  - What actions were taken  

---

## DLP Overview Dashboard:

### Purpose:
- Provides a **high-level summary** of DLP insights  

---

### Key Sections:

---

### 1. Protect Sensitive Info
- Shows:
  - Number of unprotected documents  
- Categories:
  - Financial  
  - Healthcare  
  - HR  
  - Intellectual property  
  - Customer data  

---

#### Options:
- **Get Started**
  - Helps configure DLP policies  
- **View Detected Documents**
  - Opens **Content Explorer** to analyze files  

---

### 2. Content Explorer
- Displays:
  - Actual documents matching DLP conditions  
- Enables:
  - Deep investigation of sensitive files  

---

### 3. Auto-Labeling Integration
- Extend DLP to:
  - Automatically apply sensitivity labels  

---

#### Benefits:
- Consistent protection  
- Automated classification  

---

### 4. Teams Protection
- Protect files shared in:
  - Teams chats and channels  

✅ Detects:
- New incidents  
- Sensitive data exposure  

---

## Data Flow for DLP Results:

1. User activity occurs  
2. DLP policy evaluates content  
3. Alerts generated (if matched)  
4. Data stored in:
   - Audit logs  
5. Processed and displayed in:
   - Alerts dashboard  
   - Activity explorer  
   - Reports  

---

## Why Monitoring DLP Results Matters:
- ✅ Improve policy accuracy  
- ✅ Reduce false positives  
- ✅ Identify risky behavior  
- ✅ Strengthen compliance posture  
- ✅ Support investigations  

---

## Best Practices:
- ✅ Regularly review alerts  
- ✅ Use Activity Explorer for investigations  
- ✅ Tune policies based on findings  
- ✅ Export reports for deeper analysis  
- ✅ Use dashboards for continuous monitoring  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview DLP reporting tools provide **end-to-end visibility into data protection**, enabling organizations to:
- Monitor policy effectiveness  
- Investigate incidents  
- Improve data security strategies  

➡️ These tools transform raw DLP data into **actionable insights for better compliance and risk management**
