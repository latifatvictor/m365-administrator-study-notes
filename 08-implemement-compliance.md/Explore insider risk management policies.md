## Insider Risk Management Policies – Summary

### Overview:
Insider Risk Management policies define:
- ✅ **Who is monitored** (users/groups in scope)
- ✅ **What risks are detected** (risk indicators)
- ✅ **How alerts are generated** (thresholds, scoring)

They enable organizations to **customize detection, prioritize risks, and control alert frequency**.

---

## Key Policy Capabilities:

### 1. Scope (Users in Policy)
- Apply to:
  - All users  
  - Specific users/groups  
- Can manually add users if needed  

---

### 2. Risk Indicators
- Activities that trigger alerts, such as:
  - File downloads  
  - Data sharing  
  - Printing  
  - Security changes  

✅ Configurable thresholds:
- Adjust sensitivity of alerts  

---

### 3. Content Priorities
- Focus monitoring on:
  - Specific SharePoint sites  
  - Teams channels  
  - Sensitivity labels  
  - Sensitive info types  

---

### 4. Risk Score Boosters & Anomalies
- Highlight:
  - High-risk user behavior  
  - Unusual activity patterns  

---

### 5. Policy Windows
- Define:
  - Monitoring timeframes  
  - Policy duration  

---

## Policies Dashboard (Policies Tab):

### Key Fields:
- Policy name
