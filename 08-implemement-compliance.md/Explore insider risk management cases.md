## Insider Risk Management Cases – Summary

### Overview:
- **Cases** are the central investigation unit in Insider Risk Management  
- Created from **alerts** for deeper investigation  
- Each case is:
  - Scoped to **one user**
  - Can include **multiple alerts**

---

## Purpose of Cases:
- Investigate:
  - User risk activity history  
  - Alert details  
  - Risk patterns (sequences)  
  - Associated content (files/emails)  
- Enable:
  - Collaboration  
  - Documentation  
  - Decision-making  

---

## Actions Investigators Can Take:
- ✅ Send warning/notice to user  
- ✅ Mark case as benign  
- ✅ Share case with stakeholders  
- ✅ Escalate to **eDiscovery (Premium)**  
- ✅ Resolve case  

---

## Case Investigation Areas (Tabs):

---

### 1. Case Overview
- Summary details:
  - Case status  
  - User risk score  
  - User info (department, manager, email)  

✅ Also shows:
- Associated alerts
- Alert severity & timing  

---

### 2. Alerts Tab
- Lists alerts linked to the case:
  - Status  
  - Severity  
  - Time detected  

✅ Actions:
- Filter alerts  
- Open alert details  

---

### 3. User Activity
- Visual timeline of:
  - Risk activities  
  - Sequences of events  

✅ Helps investigators:
- Understand behavior patterns  
- Analyze risk trends  

---

### 4. Activity Explorer
- Deep-dive analytics tool  
- Shows:
  - Detailed activity logs  
  - Filtered risk indicators  

✅ Used for:
- Detailed investigation  
- Event-level analysis  

---

### 5. Content Explorer
- Displays:
  - Files and emails related to alerts  

✅ Example:
- Shows files downloaded during suspicious activity  

✅ Powerful for:
- Evidence review  
- Data exposure validation  

---

### 6. Case Notes
- Record:
  - Investigator comments  
  - Findings  
  - Actions taken  

⚠️ Notes are:
- Permanent  
- Not editable/deletable  

---

### 7. Contributors
- Add additional reviewers  
- Control access to case  

✅ Roles:
- Analysts & investigators have access  
- Only investigators can view content  

---

## Case Actions:

---

### 1. Send Email Notice
- Notify user about behavior  
- Can:
  - Provide guidance  
  - Link to training  

⚠️ Does NOT close case automatically  

---

### 2. Escalate to eDiscovery (Premium)
- Used for:
  - Legal investigation  
  - Advanced review  

✅ Enables:
- Data preservation  
- Legal workflows  

---

### 3. Resolve Case
- Finalize investigation  

#### Classification:
- **Benign** → no real risk  
- **Confirmed policy violation** → actual risk  

✅ Outcome:
- Case status → Closed  
- Resolution added to notes  

---

## Investigation Workflow:
1. Alert generated  
2. Case created  
3. Investigate:
   - Activity  
   - Content  
   - Patterns  
4. Take action:
   - Notify  
   - Escalate  
5. Resolve case  

---

## Key Benefits:
- ✅ Centralized investigation hub  
- ✅ Connects alerts, activities, and content  
- ✅ Supports legal escalation  
- ✅ Improves decision-making and accountability  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Insider Risk Management cases are the **core investigation engine**, enabling organizations to:
- Analyze user behavior  
- Review evidence  
- Collaborate securely  
- Take appropriate action  

➡️ They transform alerts into **structured investigations**, ensuring effective risk mitigation and compliance management
