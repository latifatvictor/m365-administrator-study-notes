## Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager Summary

### Overview:
- **Compliance Manager** helps organizations:
  - Assess compliance posture  
  - Manage risks  
  - Track improvement actions  
  - Meet regulatory requirements  

---

## Key Benefits:
- ✅ Simplifies compliance management  
- ✅ Provides risk-based insights  
- ✅ Offers step-by-step improvement guidance  
- ✅ Helps prepare for audits  
- ✅ Tracks regulatory alignment  

---

## Compliance Score:
- A **risk-based score** showing compliance posture  
- Based on:
  - Completed improvement actions  
- Helps:
  - Prioritize high-risk areas  
  - Measure progress over time  

✅ Initial score based on:
- **Microsoft 365 data protection baseline**

---

## Key Elements:

### 1. Controls
- Define requirements for compliance

Types:
- **Microsoft-managed controls**
  - Handled by Microsoft  
- **Customer-managed controls**
  - Responsibility of your organization  
- **Shared controls**
  - Joint responsibility  

---

### 2. Assessments
- Group of controls tied to a regulation/standard  
- Example:
  - ISO 27001  
  - GDPR  

Components:
- In-scope services  
- Controls  
- Assessment score  

✅ Helps:
- Track compliance with specific regulations  

---

### 3. Templates
- Prebuilt frameworks for assessments  
- Can:
  - Use built-in templates  
  - Customize templates  
  - Create new ones  

✅ Covers:
- 300+ global regulations and standards  

---

### 4. Improvement Actions
- Tasks to improve compliance posture  
- Include:
  - Implementation steps  
  - Testing requirements  
  - Documentation storage  

✅ Can:
- Assign to users  
- Track progress  
- Store evidence  

---

## Dashboard Features:
- Displays:
  - Compliance score  
  - Risk areas  
  - Recommended actions  
- Provides:
  - Centralized view of compliance status  

---

## Premium Assessments Trial:
- 90-day trial  
- Helps:
  - Activate relevant assessments  
  - Align with industry/region standards  
- Based on:
  - Industry  
  - Location  

---

## Automated Testing:

### Purpose:
- Automatically verify improvement actions  

### Sources:
- Data lifecycle management  
- DLP  
- Information protection  
- Insider risk management  
- Secure Score  

---

### Benefits:
- ✅ Saves time  
- ✅ Updates compliance score automatically  
- ✅ Improves efficiency  

---

### Configuration:
- On by default  
- Can:
  - Enable/disable globally or per action  

---

## User History Management:

### Features:
- Track:
  - User activities  
  - Assigned actions  
  - Uploaded evidence  

### Actions:
- Export reports  
- Reassign improvement actions  
- Delete user history  

⚠️ Note:
- Deleting history is **permanent**

---

## Permissions:
- Required roles:
  - Global Administrator  
  - Compliance Manager Administrator  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager provides a **centralized, automated, and risk-based approach to compliance**, enabling organizations to **track, manage, and improve their compliance posture efficiently while aligning with global standards and regulations**.
