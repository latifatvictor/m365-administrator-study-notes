## Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager Dashboard Summary

### Overview:
The **Compliance Manager dashboard** provides a centralized view to:
- ✅ Track compliance score  
- ✅ Identify risks and gaps  
- ✅ Take guided improvement actions  

---

## Key Sections of the Dashboard:

---

### 1. Overall Compliance Score
- Displays a **percentage score** at the top
- Based on:
  - Completed improvement actions  
  - Microsoft-managed controls  
- Initial score:
  - Based on **Microsoft 365 data protection baseline**

✅ Purpose:
- Measure compliance posture  
- Track progress over time  

---

### 2. Key Improvement Actions
- Lists **top recommended actions**
- Focuses on:
  - Actions that have **highest impact** on score  

✅ Helps prioritize:
- Quick wins  
- High-risk gaps  

---

### 3. Solutions That Affect Your Score
- Highlights solutions (e.g., DLP, Information Protection)
- Shows:
  - Number of pending improvement actions  

✅ Identifies:
- Areas needing attention  
- Where compliance improvements can be made  

---

### 4. Compliance Score Breakdown

#### Two Views:
- **Categories**
  - Example:
    - Protect information  
    - Manage devices  
- **Assessments**
  - Based on:
    - Regulations (e.g., ISO, GDPR)

✅ Helps:
- Identify strengths  
- Detect weak areas needing improvement  

---

### 5. Filtering the Dashboard
- Filter by:
  - Regulations  
  - Solutions  
  - Assessment groups  
  - Data categories  

✅ Effect:
- Updates score dynamically in real time  
- Shows **focused compliance insights**  

---

## Tabs in Compliance Manager:

---

### 6. Improvement Actions Tab
- Displays all actions required for compliance

#### Key Data Points:
- Product  
- Points achieved  
- Regulations  
- Category  
- Assigned user  
- Test status  

---

#### Test Status Types:
- Not assessed  
- Passed ✅  
- Failed (low/medium/high risk)  
- Partially tested  
- Out of scope  
- Could not detect  

✅ Only **Passed actions count toward score**

---

#### Action Types:
- **Technical** → Configured in Microsoft 365 tools  
- **Nontechnical** → Policy/process-based actions  

---

### 7. Solutions Tab
- Shows compliance contribution by solution

#### Includes:
- Points achieved  
- Total possible points  
- Remaining actions  

✅ Enables:
- Direct navigation to solutions  
- Focus on high-impact areas  

---

### 8. Assessments Tab
- Lists all compliance assessments

#### Shows:
- Status:
  - Complete  
  - Incomplete  
  - In progress  
- Progress percentage  
- Improvement actions  
- Related regulations
