## Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention (DLP) – Summary

### Overview:
- Microsoft Purview Data Loss Prevention (DLP) helps organizations:
  - ✅ Detect sensitive data  
  - ✅ Prevent data loss and misuse  
  - ✅ Enforce compliance policies  
- Protects data across:
  - Cloud, on-premises, endpoints, and apps  

---

## Why DLP Matters:

### 1. Compliance
- Helps meet regulatory requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA)
- Avoids legal penalties  

---

### 2. Reputation Protection
- Prevents data breaches  
- Maintains customer trust  

---

### 3. Intellectual Property Protection
- Protects:
  - Trade secrets  
  - Patents  
  - Confidential data  

---

### 4. Cost Savings
- Avoids:
  - Breach-related costs  
  - Legal expenses  

---

## Where DLP Applies:

### Microsoft 365 Workloads:
- ✅ Exchange Online (emails)
- ✅ SharePoint Online
- ✅ OneDrive
- ✅ Microsoft Teams  

---

### Other Locations:
- ✅ Office apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- ✅ Endpoints (Windows, macOS)
- ✅ Non-Microsoft cloud apps
- ✅ On-premises file shares
- ✅ Power BI  

---

## How DLP Works:

### Detection Methods:
- Keyword matching  
- Regular expressions  
- Pattern validation  
- Context/proximity analysis  
- Machine learning  

✅ Deep content analysis (not just simple scans)

---

## Key Capabilities:

### 1. Monitor Data:
- Data at rest  
- Data in motion  
- Data in use  

---

### 2. Detect Sensitive Info:
- Credit card numbers  
- Personal data (PII)  
- Health data  
- Financial information  

---

### 3. Take Protective Actions:

#### Examples:
- ⚠️ Show policy tips (warnings)
- 🚫 Block sharing (with/without override)
- 🔒 Restrict access
- 📦 Move files to secure/quarantine location
- 🚫 Block Teams messages with sensitive data  

---

## Alerts & Reporting:
- Alerts generated for risky actions  
- Managed via:
  - ✅ DLP Alerts Dashboard  
- Enables:
  - Investigation  
  - Triage  
  - Monitoring  

---

## DLP Lifecycle:

---

### Phase 1: Plan
- Define:
  - Sensitive data types  
  - Business processes  
  - Risk scenarios  

#### Consider:
- Data locations  
- User behavior  
- Policy actions  

✅ Includes:
- Technology planning  
- Business process alignment  
- User training  

---

### Phase 2: Prepare
- Configure:
  - Data sources (Exchange, SharePoint, etc.)  
  - Required integrations  
- Create:
  - Draft policies  
- Test:
  - Before enforcement  

---

### Phase 3: Deploy

#### Step 1: Design Policies
- Define control objectives  
- Apply to workloads  

---

#### Step 2: Run Policies

**Simulation Mode:**
- ✅ No enforcement  
- ✅ Test safely  

**Enforce Mode:**
- ✅ Active protection  
- ✅ Actions applied  

---

#### Step 3: Monitor & Tune
- Adjust:
  - Conditions  
  - Scope  
  - Sensitivity definitions  
- Ensure:
  - Minimal business disruption  

---

## Best Practices:
- ✅ Start in **Simulation mode**  
- ✅ Fine-tune before enforcement  
- ✅ Train users using policy tips  
- ✅ Monitor continuously  
- ✅ Adjust policies over time  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Microsoft Purview DLP provides **comprehensive protection across multiple workloads** by:
- Detecting sensitive data  
- Monitoring user activity  
- Enforcing policies  

➡️ Ensuring **data security, regulatory compliance, and risk reduction across the organization**
