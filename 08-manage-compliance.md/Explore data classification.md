## Data Classification in Microsoft Purview – Summary

### Overview:
- **Data classification** is the process of:
  - Identifying  
  - Categorizing  
  - Protecting data based on sensitivity  

👉 Purpose:
- Prevent data:
  - Leakage  
  - Misuse  
  - Unauthorized access  

---

## Why Data Classification Matters:
- ✅ Protects sensitive business data  
- ✅ Reduces risk of breaches  
- ✅ Supports compliance (legal/regulatory)  
- ✅ Maintains reputation and trust  
- ✅ Prioritizes protection efforts  

---

## Ways to Classify Data in Microsoft Purview:
- Sensitivity labels  
- Retention labels  
- Sensitive information types  
- Trainable classifiers  

---

## Data Classification Framework:

### Definition:
- A structured policy defining:
  - Data sensitivity levels  
  - Protection controls  

---

### Typical Classification Levels:
- Public  
- General/Internal  
- Confidential  
- Highly Confidential  

---

### Naming Best Practice:
- Use clear names:
  - ✅ “Confidential” vs ❌ “Restricted” (ambiguous)
  - ✅ “Highly Confidential” vs “Confidential” (clear hierarchy)

---

## Example Level:

### Highly Confidential:
- 🔒 Most sensitive data  
- Requires strict controls  

#### Examples:
- Personal data (PII)  
- Financial data  
- Health records (PHI)  
- Bank/account details  

---

## Controls by Classification Level:

### Examples of Controls:
- Access restrictions  
- Encryption  
- Storage limitations  
- Data sharing rules
