## Trainable Classifiers in Microsoft Purview – Summary

### Overview:
Trainable classifiers are **AI/ML-based classification tools** in Microsoft Purview that:
- ✅ Learn from examples (not just patterns)
- ✅ Identify content based on **context and meaning**
- ✅ Classify data that pattern-based rules can’t detect

---

## 3 Ways Microsoft 365 Classifies Content:

### 1. Manual Classification
- Users/admins apply labels manually  
- Requires human judgment  

---

### 2. Pattern-Based Classification
- Detects:
  - Keywords  
  - Regex patterns  
  - Sensitive info (e.g., SSNs, credit cards)  
- Works well for structured data  

---

### 3. Trainable Classifiers ✅
- Uses machine learning  
- Trained using sample content  
- Identifies:
  - Content type (not just data patterns)  

👉 Example:
- Detects a **contract or resume** rather than specific keywords  

---

## Why Use Trainable Classifiers:
- ✅ Handles unstructured data  
- ✅ Detects semantic meaning
