# Examine How Attackers Delete Data From Your Tenant

## Overview

Data deletion attacks occur when attackers intentionally remove, encrypt, or destroy organizational data, often making recovery difficult or impossible. Data deletion attacks can severely damage organizations through ransomware, credential theft, and unauthorized deletions. The most effective defenses include MFA, least privilege access, offline backups, monitoring, encryption, patch management, and layered security controls.

These attacks can severely impact:
- Business operations
- Productivity
- Compliance
- Reputation
- Financial stability

---

# What is Data Deletion?

Data deletion refers to:
- Unauthorized destruction or removal of organizational data.

Attackers may target:
- Emails
- Documents
- SharePoint sites
- OneDrive files
- Teams data
- Databases
- Backup repositories
