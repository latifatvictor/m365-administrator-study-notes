# Explore Today's Work and Threat Landscape

## Overview

Modern cybersecurity threats continue to evolve as attackers become more sophisticated and organizations increasingly adopt cloud technologies. Businesses today must secure users, devices, applications, and data across hybrid environments that include:

- On-premises systems
- Cloud services
- Mobile devices
- Remote users
- Third-party applications

Microsoft 365 helps organizations protect against, detect, and respond to modern threat vectors through layered security controls and cloud-based protection services. Today's cybersecurity landscape is significantly more complex due to cloud adoption, remote work, mobile devices, and sophisticated cyberattacks. Organizations must secure identities, devices, applications, and data across hybrid environments while maintaining productivity and compliance. Microsoft 365 helps organizations defend against modern threats by providing layered security, identity protection, monitoring, and threat response capabilities designed to protect against evolving attack techniques.
