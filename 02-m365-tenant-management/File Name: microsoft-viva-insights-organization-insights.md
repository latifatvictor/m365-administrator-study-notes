# Explore Organization Insights in Microsoft Viva Insights

# Overview

Organization insights in Microsoft Viva Insights help business leaders and managers understand:

- Employee wellbeing
- Productivity trends
- Team culture
- Collaboration effectiveness
- Workplace behaviour patterns

The feature provides actionable analytics that help organizations improve workplace health and performance.

---

# Purpose of Organization Insights

Organization insights help leaders:

- Understand how teams are working
- Identify burnout risks
- Improve meeting culture
- Increase focus time
- Strengthen collaboration
- Support employee wellbeing
- Improve productivity

---

# Who Can Access Organization Insights?

To access Organization insights, users must meet ALL required conditions.

---

# Requirements for Access

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| Viva Insights subscription | Premium Viva Insights license required |
| Assigned role | Must be assigned Insights Business Leader role OR enabled as Group Manager |
| Minimum group size | Reporting group must meet minimum size configured by administrator |
| Measured employees | Employees must also have Viva Insights licenses |

---

# Important

Employees included in Organization insights are called:

- Measured employees

---

# Measured Employee Requirements

Employees must:

- Have Viva Insights premium licensing
- Be active in Outlook or Teams
- Be part of the reporting hierarchy

---

# Full Organization Visibility

If leaders want insights covering the entire organization:

- Every employee must have a Viva Insights subscription.

---

# Types of Organization Insights Access

There are two major access models.

---

# 1. Insights Business Leader

Users assigned the Insights Business Leader role can view:

- Organization-wide insights
- Company-wide analytics
- Enterprise productivity trends

These insights include:

- All measured employees in the tenant.

---

# 2. Group Manager

Users enabled as Group Managers can view:

- Insights for employees reporting directly or indirectly to them.

---

# Important

Administrators maintain the reporting hierarchy.

---

# Dual Access Example

A user can simultaneously be:

- Insights Business Leader
AND
- Group Manager

In this case:

- The user can toggle between:
  - Entire company insights
  - Reporting organization insights

---

# Privacy in Organization Insights

Microsoft Viva Insights strongly protects employee privacy.

---

# Core Privacy Principle

No user can discover information about another identified individual that they could not already know.

---

# Privacy Features

| Privacy Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Aggregated analytics | Data shown as trends |
| No employee surveillance | No individual monitoring |
| Minimum group size protection | Prevents identifying individuals |
| Metadata-based analysis | Uses collaboration metadata only |

---

# Important

Organization insights do NOT expose:

- Personal emails
- Message contents
- Private conversations
- Individual employee behaviour

# Organization Insights Metrics

The platform measures several workplace behaviour metrics.

---

# Metric Definitions

## After-Hours Collaboration Hours

Measures:

- Time spent working outside configured working hours

Includes:

- Meetings
- Emails
- Teams chats
- Teams calls

---

# Uninterrupted Focus Hours

Measures:

- Time blocks longer than one hour without interruptions

Interruptions include:

- Meetings
- Calls
- Emails
- Teams chats

---

# Daily Connected Hours

Measures:

- 30-minute periods with collaboration activity

Includes:

- Meetings
- Calls
- Emails
- Chats

---

# Collaboration Hours

Measures:

- Total weekly collaboration time

Includes:

- Meetings
- Emails
- Calls
- Chats

---

# Meeting Hours

Measures:

- Weekly time spent in meetings

Based on:

- Accepted Outlook meetings

Excludes:

- Appointments
- Nonmeeting calendar items

---

# Join On Time Rate

Measures:

- Percentage of Teams meetings joined on time

Definition of on time:

- Early
OR
- Within five minutes of scheduled start

---

# Recurring Meeting Hours

Measures:

- Weekly time spent in recurring meetings

---

# Large and Long Meeting Hours

Measures:

- Weekly time spent in:
  - Meetings longer than one hour
  - Meetings with more than eight attendees

---

# Multitasking Hours

Measures:

- Time spent emailing or chatting during meetings

---

# Manager 1:1 Meeting Hours

Measures:

- Time spent in meetings with direct manager

Calculated over:

- Rolling four-week period

---

# Internal Network Size

Measures:

- Number of colleague connections

Based on:

- At least two meaningful interactions

Excludes:

- Large or lengthy interactions

---

# Manager Coattendance Rate

Measures:

- Percentage of meetings attended by both employee and direct manager

---
