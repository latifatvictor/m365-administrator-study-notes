## Threat Analytics in Microsoft 365 Summary

### Overview:
- Microsoft’s **built-in threat intelligence solution**
- Helps security teams detect, understand, and respond to **emerging threats**
- Focuses on:
  - Active attack campaigns
  - New attack techniques
  - Vulnerabilities
  - Malware and attack surfaces

---

### Key Goals:
- Identify and respond to threats quickly  
- Determine if the organization is under attack  
- Assess impact and exposure  
- Provide **recommended mitigation actions**  

---

### Threat Types Prioritized:
- **High-impact threats:** Greatest potential damage  
- **High-exposure threats:** Highest vulnerability risk to your environment  

---

## Threat Analytics Dashboard:
Provides a centralized view of relevant threats:

- **Latest threats:** Recently discovered or updated threats  
- **High-impact threats:** Most damaging threats  
- **Highest exposure threats:** Based on:
  - Vulnerability severity
  - Number of affected devices  

---

## Threat Analytics Report (Key Tabs):

### 1. Overview:
- Summarizes threat impact and exposure
- Key charts:
  - Related incidents (active alerts & severity)
  - Alerts over time
  - Impacted assets (devices & mailboxes)
  - Prevented email attempts
