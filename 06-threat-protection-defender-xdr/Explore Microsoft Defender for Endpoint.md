## Microsoft Defender for Endpoint Summary

### Overview:
- Enterprise **endpoint security platform** for:
  - Prevention
  - Detection
  - Investigation
  - Response to advanced threats

---

## Core Technologies:
- **Endpoint behavioral sensors**
  - Built into Windows 10/11
  - Collect system activity data

- **Cloud security analytics**
  - Uses AI, ML, and big data
  - Converts signals into alerts and insights

- **Threat intelligence**
  - Microsoft + partner intelligence
  - Detects attacker tools, tactics, techniques (TTPs)

---

## Key Capabilities:

### 1. Threat & Vulnerability Management
- Identifies and prioritizes vulnerabilities
- Provides remediation guidance

---

### 2. Attack Surface Reduction
- Reduces exposure to threats
- Includes:
  - Network protection
  - Web protection

---

### 3. Next-Generation Protection
- Advanced antivirus and anti-malware
- Protects against emerging threats

---

### 4. Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)
- Detects and investigates advanced attacks
- Includes:
  - Advanced hunting (query-based searches)

---

### 5. Automated Investigation & Remediation (AIR)
- Automatically investigates alerts
- Executes or recommends fixes

---

### 6. Microsoft Secure Score for Devices
- Assesses endpoint security posture
- Provides improvement recommendations

---

### 7. Microsoft Threat Experts (Add-on)
- Managed threat hunting service
- Provides:
  - Proactive insights
  - Targeted attack notifications

---
