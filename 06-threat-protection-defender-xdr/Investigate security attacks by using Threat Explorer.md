## Threat Explorer (Microsoft Defender for Office 365) Summary

### Overview:
- Advanced **threat investigation tool** (Plan 2 feature)
- Plan 1 alternative: **Real-time detections** (limited)
- Used by security teams to:
  - Investigate attacks
  - Analyze emails and files
  - Take remediation actions

---

## Key Capabilities:
- View:
  - Malware detections
  - Phishing URLs and verdicts
  - Malicious emails (delivered or blocked)
  - Files in OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams  
- Perform actions:
  - Trigger **Automated Investigation & Response (AIR)**
  - Delete emails
- Analyze:
  - Email headers and bodies
  - Email timelines
- Export:
  - Email and URL data (up to 200,000 records)

---

## Main Views:

- **All Email (Explorer only)**
  - Shows all emails (good + malicious)
  - Up to **30 days** of data

- **Malware View**
  - Emails flagged with malware

- **Phish View**
  - Emails flagged as phishing

- **Content Malware View**
  - Malicious files in:
    - SharePoint
    - OneDrive
    - Teams

---

## Core Components:

### 1. Filters
- Filter by:
  - Sender, recipient, subject
  - URLs, attachments, threat type
- Supports:
  - AND logic (default)
  - OR logic (advanced)

---

### 2. Charts
- Visual summary of threats
- Shows aggregated trends based on filters

---

### 3. Results Grid
- Detailed list of emails/files
- Customizable columns
- Drill down into specific items

---

### 4. Detailed Panes
- View deeper insights for:
  - Emails
  - URLs
  - IP addresses
  - Recipients
- Includes:
  - Email summary
  - Full entity details

---

### 5. Actions
- Take remediation steps:
  - Delete email
  - Investigate threats
  - Trigger automation

---

### 6. Export
- Export:
  - Chart data
  - Email data
- Best practice: apply filters before exporting

---

## Key Insights Features:
- Top URLs
- Top clicks
- Top targeted users
- Email origin analysis

---

## Key Difference:
- **Threat Explorer (Plan 2):**
  - Full investigation + remediation + all email view  

- **Real-time detections (Plan 1):**
  - Limited visibility, no full hunting capability  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Threat Explorer provides **deep visibility and control over email threats**, enabling security teams to **investigate, analyze, and remediate attacks efficiently across the Microsoft 365 environment**.
