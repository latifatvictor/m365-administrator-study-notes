## Automated Investigation and Response (AIR) – Summary

### Overview:
- AIR in Microsoft Defender XDR automates **threat investigation and remediation**
- Helps reduce **alert overload** and improves SOC efficiency
- Triggers automatically when certain alerts or incidents occur

---

### How AIR Works:
1. Alert is generated → creates an **incident**
2. AIR runs an **automated investigation**
3. System analyzes evidence and assigns **verdicts**:
   - Malicious
   - Suspicious
   - No threats found
4. Identifies and executes or recommends **remediation actions**

---

### Remediation Actions Examples:
- Quarantine files  
- Stop processes  
- Isolate devices  
- Block URLs  

---

### Automation Modes:
- ✅ Fully automatic remediation  
- ✅ Approval-based remediation (via Action Center)  

---

### Self-Healing Capabilities:
- Automatically detects and fixes threats across:
  - Devices
  - Email & content
  - Identities
- Expands investigations if related threats are found
- Continuously adapts during active investigations

---

### Action Center:
- Central place to manage actions:
  - View pending & completed actions
  - Approve or reject remediation steps
  - Access investigation details
  - Launch advanced hunting

---

### Investigation Details View:
Provides deep visibility through tabs:

- **Graph:** Visual map of threats, entities, alerts  
- **Alerts:** Related alerts list  
- **Devices / Users / Mailboxes:** Affected assets  
- **Evidence:** Threat findings + verdicts  
- **Entities:** Detailed analysis per object  
- **Log:** Timeline of actions  
- **Pending Actions:** Items needing approval  

---

### Key Benefits:
- ✅ Faster threat detection and response  
- ✅ Reduced manual workload  
- ✅ Improved accuracy with AI-driven analysis  
- ✅ Scalable security operations  
- ✅ Better use of security tools  

---

### Key Takeaway:
AIR enables **automated, intelligent, and scalable threat response**, allowing security teams to focus on **high-value tasks instead of manual investigations**.
