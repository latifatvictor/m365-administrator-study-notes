## Attack Simulation Training – Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Summary

### Overview:
- Available in:
  - **Microsoft 365 E5**
  - **Defender for Office 365 Plan 2**
- Simulates real-world cyberattacks to:
  - Identify vulnerable users
  - Improve security awareness
  - Test organizational defenses

---

## Purpose:
- Detect weak points in user behavior  
- Train users against phishing and attacks  
- Improve overall **security posture and readiness**

---

## Prerequisites:
- Required license (E5 or Plan 2)  
- Required roles:
  - Global Administrator  
  - Security Administrator  
  - Attack Simulator Admin / Payload Author  

---

## Key Insights:
- **Actual compromise rate:** % of users who failed simulation  
- **Predicted compromise rate:** benchmark vs other organizations  
- Helps track improvement over time  

---

## Simulation Process:

### 1. Select Attack Technique
- Credential harvest (fake login pages)  
- Malware attachment (malicious files)  
- Link in attachment  
- Link to malware (hosted files)  
- Drive-by URL (background malicious code)

---

### 2. Select Payload
- Prebuilt or custom attack templates
- Metrics include:
  - Click rate  
  - Predicted compromise rate  
  - Complexity  
  - Usage frequency  

---

### 3. Audience Targeting
- All users or selected groups
- Import users via CSV or filters

---

### 4. Assign Training
- Automatically assign training modules
- Set due dates
- Improves user resilience to attacks

---

### 5. Launch & Review
- Run immediately or schedule
- Supports region-based delivery timing
- Review and adjust before final submission

---

## End-User Notifications:
- Sent based on simulation results:
  - Positive reinforcement
  - Simulation notification
  - Training assignment
  - Training reminders
- Types:
  - Global (default templates)
  - Tenant (custom)

---

## Benefits:
- ✅ Identifies high-risk users  
- ✅ Improves employee awareness  
- ✅ Strengthens defense against phishing  
- ✅ Tests security policies in real scenarios  
- ✅ Tracks training effectiveness  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Attack Simulation Training helps organizations **proactively prepare for cyberattacks** by combining **realistic simulations with user training**, reducing the likelihood of successful phishing and social engineering attacks.
