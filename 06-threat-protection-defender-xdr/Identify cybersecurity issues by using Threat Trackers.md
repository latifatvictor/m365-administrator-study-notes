## Threat Trackers – Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Summary

### Overview:
- **Threat Trackers** provide actionable **threat intelligence dashboards**
- Help security teams:
  - Identify emerging threats
  - Track campaigns
  - Investigate trends
- Accessible via:
  - Defender portal → Email & collaboration → Threat tracker

---

## Key Features:

### 1. Noteworthy Trackers
- Highlights **important/new threats** (e.g., ransomware campaigns)
- Includes:
  - Real-world attack examples
  - Links to detailed analysis
- Short-lived (removed once threat risk declines)
- ✅ Focus on **high-priority, current threats**

---

### 2. Trending Trackers
- Shows **new or increasing threats**
- Identifies campaigns not seen in the past week
- Helps organizations:
  - Stay proactive
  - Prepare defenses

---

### 3. Tracked Queries
- Automatically runs saved searches
- Provides **continuous monitoring + trend analysis**
- No need to manually re-run queries

---

### 4. Saved Queries
- Stores commonly used **Threat Explorer searches**
- Enables faster investigations
- Can reuse anytime

---

## Key Components:
- **Trending metrics** (updated regularly)  
- **Widgets** showing biggest/growing issues  
- **Quick links** to Threat Explorer for deep investigation  

---

## Integration with Threat Explorer:
- Threat Trackers → identify threats  
- Threat Explorer → investigate in detail  
- Together enable:
  - Detection
  - Analysis
  - Response

---

## Example Use Case:
- Identify a trending phishing campaign in Tracker  
- Click link → open in Threat Explorer  
- Analyze:
  - Targeted users
  - Email content
- Take action:
  - Remove emails
  - Trigger remediation  

---

## Advanced Insights:
- Detect threats via:
  - Safe Attachments (sandbox detonation)
  - Behavioral analysis
  - Heuristics
- Review details via **Advanced Analysis tab** (for malicious attachments)

---

## Permissions Required:
- Global Administrator  
- Security Administrator  
- Security Reader  

---

## Key Takeaway:
Threat Trackers provide **high-level threat visibility and trend intelligence**, helping organizations **quickly identify and prioritize emerging threats**, while Threat Explorer enables **deep investigation and response**.
